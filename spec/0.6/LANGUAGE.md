# ModelLang 0.6 — Normative Language

Status: reference-compiler specification. Rules listed in `UNSTABLE.md` carry no compatibility guarantee.

ModelLang 0.6 consists of the complete [ModelLang 0.5 language](../0.5/LANGUAGE.md) plus the [0.6 declaration identity and rename semantics](./DECLARATION_IDENTITY.md).

Where 0.6 differs from an earlier version, the 0.6 rule takes precedence. The [grammar](./GRAMMAR.ebnf) extends 0.5, and [conformance](./CONFORMANCE.md) is cumulative.
