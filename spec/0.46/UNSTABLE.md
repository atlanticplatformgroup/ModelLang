# ModelLang 0.46 unstable boundaries

The following remain outside the stable 0.46 contract:

- favorable claims about agent quality, planning improvement, productivity, safety, latency, token use, or superiority over integrated existing specifications;
- a bundled model provider, prompt, credential manager, network runner, sampling strategy, repeated-trial controller, statistical analysis, leaderboard, or benchmark publication service;
- treating a synthetic replay, deterministic scorer, generated assessment, discovered tool, applicability result, packet, trace, resource, or extension result as authority or empirical evidence;
- complete effect, reversibility, compensation, recovery, task-goal, observation-relevance, or semantic closure;
- generated or verified extension implementations, host evidence attestation, delegated extension authority, resource templates or subscriptions, prompts, MCP Tasks, historical/full traces, transferable/chained delegation, complete SML-Agent conformance, and SML-Federation conformance.

Future empirical claims require a separately controlled evaluation whose raw observations, runner configuration, repetitions, exclusions, and uncertainty are reviewable. A favorable comparison must outperform the integrated-specification condition, not merely prose or unstructured source.
