# ModelLang 0.29 unstable boundaries

The following remain outside the stable contract: public or agent claiming, assigning another operator, release, reassignment, transfer, delegation, claim leases or expiry, workload balancing, claimant discovery through observation, approval workflows, notifications, alerts, dashboards, batch claims, automated claiming or recovery, payload inspection, broker introspection, retention, cross-model operations, and distributed tracing.

A claim records the first authenticated database operator to claim one current PostgreSQL-local terminal cycle. It does not prove acknowledgement, investigation, remediation, active work, ownership outside that cycle, broker state, or eventual recovery. A claim, generation, observer Boolean, or record grants no authority.

The stable guarantee is isolated, immutable, first-writer self-claiming serialized with recovery and minimally reflected in private observation. It is not a queue administration system, transferable assignment tracker, approval workflow, notification system, or general operations console.
