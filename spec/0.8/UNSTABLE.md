# ModelLang 0.8 unstable money boundaries

The following remain undefined:

- user-declared currencies and custom precision or scale;
- a comprehensive or automatically updated currency registry;
- currency aliases, redenomination, and profile migration;
- addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, allocation, percentages, tax, and discounts;
- rounding modes and rounding operations;
- exchange rates, conversions, rate timestamps, and market-data sources;
- locale-aware parsing, formatting, symbols, and display policy;
- money aggregation and computed fields;
- mixed-currency collections and totals;
- automatic migration from `Decimal` to `Money<C>`.
